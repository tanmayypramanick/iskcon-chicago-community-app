Your account

The app is meant for devotees and well-wishers taking part in the ISKCON Chicago community, and for adults — accounts are not for children under 13. Use your own account, with your own name, and give us information that is true, including a current phone number.

How to behave in it

This is a temple's community space. Speak to devotees the way you would in the temple room.

Please do not

  • Harass, threaten or abuse anybody, or post unlawful, hateful or obscene content.
  • Impersonate another devotee, or use somebody else's account.
  • Advertise, solicit, sell or raise funds for anything of your own.
  • Pass on another devotee's private information, or photograph or name a child without their parent's agreement.
  • Register for seva you do not intend to perform, or record seva time, attendance or verification dishonestly.
  • Try to break, overload or get around the app's security, or reach records you have not been given access to.
  • Copy the Directory or any part of the temple's records for use outside the community.

If your availability for a seva changes, update the app or tell the coordinator as early as you can, so somebody else can cover it.

What you share

What you write and the photos you post stay yours. By posting them you give ISKCON Chicago permission to store, display and keep them within the app as part of running the community and its records.

Share only what you are entitled to share. Messages, sanga chats and photos are kept as temple records, and specifically authorised leadership can read them; deleting a message removes it from the conversation but does not destroy the record. The Privacy Policy explains this in full, and it is worth reading before you write something you would not want kept.
